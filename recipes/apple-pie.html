Apple Pie Recipe

Description: A classic American apple pie with a flaky crust and spiced apple filling.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and diced
  • 1/4 cup ice water
  • 6 cups thinly sliced apples (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp)
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Gradually add ice water, tossing with a fork until dough holds together.
  2. Divide dough in half, shape into disks, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  4. Roll out one disk of dough on a floured surface to fit a 9-inch pie pan. Transfer to the pan and trim excess dough.
  5. In a large bowl, combine sliced apples, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice. Toss to coat.
  6. Pour apple mixture into the prepared crust.
  7. Roll out the second disk of dough and place over the filling. Trim and crimp edges. Cut slits in the top crust for steam to escape.
  8. Brush the top crust with beaten egg.
  9. Bake for 45-50 minutes or until crust is golden brown and filling is bubbly. If the edges brown too quickly, cover them with foil.
  10. Cool on a wire rack before serving.
