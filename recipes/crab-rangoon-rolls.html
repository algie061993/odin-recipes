Crab Rangoon Rolls

Description: Delicious crab and cream cheese-filled rolls wrapped in crispy wonton shells.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup cooked crab meat, chopped
  • 2 green onions, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 20 wonton wrappers
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix cream cheese, crab meat, green onions, soy sauce, garlic powder, and black pepper until well combined.
  2. Place a wonton wrapper on a clean surface. Spoon about 1 tablespoon of the crab mixture onto the center of the wrapper.
  3. Moisten the edges of the wrapper with water and fold it over to form a triangle. Press the edges to seal.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a deep fryer or large skillet to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry the wontons in batches until golden brown and crispy, about 2-3 minutes per side.
  6. Remove from oil and drain on paper towels. Serve warm with dipping sauce.
